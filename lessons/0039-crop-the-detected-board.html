Crop The Detected Board

The board detector gives coordinates. The next step is cutting that board region out of the screenshot.

Why Crop?

Champion and unit analysis should not look at the full screenshot. The full screenshot contains side UI, shop, bench, facecam, YouTube controls, and chat.

full screenshot -> detect board -> crop board -> analyze board contents

What Those Coordinates Mean

x1, y1 = top-left corner
x2, y2 = bottom-right corner

Important Detail

Image crop coordinates should be integers, so convert the float values:

left = int(detection["x1"])
top = int(detection["y1"])
right = int(detection["x2"])
bottom = int(detection["y2"])
