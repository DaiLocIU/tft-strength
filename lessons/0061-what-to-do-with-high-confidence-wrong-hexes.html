What To Do With High Confidence Wrong Hexes

When you see a wrong red hex with high confidence, do not stop and retrain immediately. Record it first. Relabel after you collect a small focused batch.

Goal: turn high-confidence mistakes into a clean mistake queue, then relabel that queue.

The Rule

High confidence means the model strongly believes the wrong thing. A threshold will not fix that. The model needs corrected examples of the same mistake type. 

low confidence wrong:
  maybe threshold can fix

high confidence wrong:
  record image, relabel, retrain

What To Do Right Now

  1. Open the prediction review sheet.
  2. When you see a wrong red hex with high confidence, write down the image number.
  3. Keep reviewing until you collect about 20 to 50 wrong images.
  4. Create a new mistake queue from those image numbers.
  5. Open the queue in the labeler and relabel those images.
  6. Rebuild the split, train again, and predict all again.

Do Not Relabel One At A Time

Relabeling one image and retraining immediately is too slow. The model learns better from a small batch of similar mistakes. 

bad:
  find 1 wrong image
  relabel 1 image
  train

good:
  find 20-50 wrong images
  relabel the queue
  train once

What To Record

For now, record only the image number. That is enough because the labeler reloads the whole board.

example:
  1, 3, 4, 22, 48, 101

If the same image has many wrong red hexes, write the image number once. When you relabel, correct all hexes on that board.

Relabel Rule

When relabeling large champion mistakes, use the standing hex rule.

click occupied:
  feet / shadow / anchor hex

do not click occupied:
  head only
  body overlap only
  item effect only

Stop Rule

If the same high-confidence mistake survives two focused relabel-and-train rounds, stop labeling more. That means the crop design probably needs to change. 

after two failed focused rounds:
  try smaller/lower crop
  or add champion detector anchor signal

Recall

  • What do you do first when a high-confidence wrong hex appears?
  • Why collect a batch before retraining?
  • What rule decides the true occupied hex?
