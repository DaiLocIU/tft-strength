Why Training Can Detect The Board

Training works by repeatedly comparing the model's guessed box to your labeled box, then adjusting the model.

One Training Example

image: TFT screenshot
label: board rectangle from you

Your label is the answer key.

Before Training

The model already has visual knowledge from pretraining, but it does not know your exact task yet.

It may notice:
- edges
- shapes
- textures
- UI regions
- repeated screen patterns

During Training

For each image, YOLO does this loop:

1. Look at the screenshot.
2. Guess where the board is.
3. Compare guess box with your label box.
4. Calculate error.
5. Adjust model numbers to reduce the error.
6. Repeat on many images.

The Important Word: Error

If the model guesses too far left, the error tells it to move the box right next time.

Your label:
[        board        ]

Model guess:
     [        board        ]

Error:
guess is too far right

What The Model Learns

It does not memorize the word "board". It learns visual patterns that usually mean board:

hex grid area
board edges
central arena shape
common TFT camera angle
relative position inside the screenshot

Why More Images Help

One image teaches one case. Many images teach what stays stable across cases.

Different maps
Different units
Different effects
Different stream overlays
Same target: board rectangle

Mental Model

label = answer key
prediction = model guess
loss = how wrong the guess is
training = changing the model to reduce loss
