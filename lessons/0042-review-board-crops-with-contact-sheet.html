Review Board Crops With A Contact Sheet

After cropping boards, do not jump straight to champion detection. First, inspect the crops quickly so bad board crops do not poison the next dataset.

Goal: create one review image that shows many board crops in a grid, with each crop's filename printed under it.

Why This Step Exists

Your detector produced 343 board crops and 5 failures. That is good, but “cropped” only means the model found a box. It does not prove the box is perfectly useful.

Before detecting champions, you want to answer one question: are most board crops clean enough that a second model can learn from them?

What A Contact Sheet Is

A contact sheet is one big image containing many small images. For your project, it is a fast visual QA tool.

Think In Small Pieces

  • Read crop files from outputs/board-crops/*.png.
  • Resize each crop into a thumbnail.
  • Create a blank white image large enough for the grid.
  • Paste each thumbnail into its cell.
  • Draw the filename below the thumbnail.

Why Filename Text Matters

If one crop is wrong, the filename tells you exactly which raw screenshot to inspect, label, or retrain with later.
