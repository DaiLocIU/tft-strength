Label The First Hex Occupancy Batch

The tiny labeler is ready. Your next job is to create the first real dataset for the classifier, not to train yet.

Goal: label 30 board crops, then check whether the saved occupied and empty patches look correct.

What To Click

Click a hex only when a champion is standing on that hex.

click:
  champion on board hex

do not click:
  empty hex
  bench unit
  shop unit
  item orb
  little legend
  mouse cursor
  health bar only

The tool defaults every hex to empty. That is good because most cells are empty. You only mark the minority class: occupied.

Exact Work Session

  1. Open http://localhost:8091.
  2. For each board crop, click only occupied hexes.
  3. Press Save labels.
  4. Stop after 30 board crops.
  5. Ask Codex to create an occupancy dataset contact sheet.

Why Stop At 30?

Thirty board crops gives about 30 x 28 = 840 patch examples with the current player-side grid. That is enough to find bad crop size, wrong hex alignment, or confusing labels before you spend time labeling 100+ boards.

What Good Data Looks Like

The saved occupied images should usually show a champion body or feet. The saved empty images should show only board/arena texture. Some difficult examples are allowed, but the folder should mostly be obvious to your eyes.

Stop Rule

Do not train until these three checks pass:

occupied folder has examples
empty folder has examples
random saved patches visually match their folder label

Recall

  • Why do we click only occupied hexes?
  • Why stop after 30 boards instead of labeling everything?
  • What must be true before training?
