Diagnose A Weak Champion Detector

Your current champion detector draws nothing at normal confidence and draws everything at very low confidence. That means it is not ready for the pipeline yet.

Next goal: find out whether the problem is training/export, or simply not enough champion examples.

The Meaning

conf=0.25:
  no boxes

conf=0.01:
  boxes everywhere

meaning:
  the model has weak guesses, not real confidence

Do This Now

  1. Test the detector on the training images, not validation.
  2. If training images also fail, inspect labels/export/training setup.
  3. If training images work but validation fails, label more varied images.
  4. Do not combine this model with hex occupancy yet.

How To Decide

case A:
  train images show good boxes
  val images show bad boxes

next:
  dataset is too small
  label 50 more board crops

case B:
  train images also show no boxes

next:
  something is wrong earlier
  check Label Studio export
  check YOLO txt labels
  train again from yolo11n.pt

If You Need More Data

What Not To Do Yet

Do not write box-to-hex mapping yet. A mapper cannot fix a detector that does not know what a champion body looks like.
