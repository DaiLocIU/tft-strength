Label 100 Board Images

Your job now is not coding. Your job is teaching the future model what board means.

Your config is correct for this stage: one image, one rectangle tool, one label named board.

The Config

<View>
  <Image name="image" value="$image"/>
  <RectangleLabels name="label" toName="image">
    <Label value="board" background="#FF0000"/>
  </RectangleLabels>
</View>

The Skill

Computer vision starts with a definition. If you label the bench sometimes and exclude it other times, the model learns confusion. So the real skill is drawing the same semantic boundary across many screenshots.

Your Boundary Rule

For this first detector, label only the combat board. Exclude bench, shop, side panels, top UI, and champion-specific boxes.

Before Labeling All 100

Label only 10 images first. Then stop and review them as a set. Ask: “Did I use the same definition of board every time?” Fix those 10 before continuing.

Why 100 Images

One or two images teach memorization. A larger set teaches variation: different units, effects, camera states, rounds, and visual clutter. That variation is what helps the detector generalize.

Mini Quiz

  2. Should every screenshot have exactly one board rectangle?
  3. Which matters more at this stage: consistent labels or perfect edges?
