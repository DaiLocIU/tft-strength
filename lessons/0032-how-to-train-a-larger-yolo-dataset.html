How To Train A Larger YOLO Dataset

A larger dataset needs a real train/validation split before you judge model quality.

The Folder Shape

data/yolo/images/train
data/yolo/images/val
data/yolo/labels/train
data/yolo/labels/val

The Dataset YAML

path: data/yolo
train: images/train
val: images/val

names:
  0: board

The Split

For a simple first version, use about 80% for training and 20% for validation.

100 images -> 80 train, 20 val
300 images -> 240 train, 60 val
500 images -> 400 train, 100 val

Why This Matters

Training images teach the model. Validation images check whether it works on screenshots it did not learn from directly.

Training Command

.venv/bin/yolo detect train \
  model=yolo11n.pt \
  data=data/yolo/dataset.yaml \
  epochs=50 \
  imgsz=640 \
  batch=8 \
  project=runs/detect \
  name=train-50

Settings To Understand

  • epochs: how many full passes through the training set.
  • imgsz: image size used during training.
  • batch: how many images are processed together.
  • device: CPU, GPU, or Apple mps.

Simple Rule

With more data, prefer better validation over just more epochs.
